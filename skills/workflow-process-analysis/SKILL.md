# Process Analysis Skill

## Purpose

- Analyze current business processes and identify areas for improvement

## Workflow

1. Document current processes step-by-step
2. Identify pain points, bottlenecks, and inefficiencies
3. Gather input from process owners and stakeholders
4. Propose improvements and alternatives
5. Summarize recommendations and expected benefits

## Best Practices

- Use data and stakeholder feedback
- Visualize processes with diagrams
- Prioritize improvements by impact and feasibility

## Quick Start

1. Invoke this skill during process review or improvement initiatives
2. Use output to guide business change and optimization
