# Principles

1. **KISS** — Prefer the simplest solution that works.
2. **Decompose backwards from the final state** — Start with the desired outcome and work backwards to the first actionable step.
3. **Smallest possible deliverable** — Ship the smallest thing that moves this forward.
4. **Single source of truth** — Every piece of knowledge lives in exactly one place.
5. **Role clarity** — Each agent has bounded responsibilities; when a task is outside your role, delegate it.
6. **Context before action** — Read and understand *why* before deciding *how*.
7. **Explicit handoffs** — State clearly what was done, what remains, and who acts next.
8. **Convention over configuration** — Follow established conventions; only deviate with a compelling reason.
9. **Separation of concerns** — Workflow logic, reference knowledge, and business rules are separate.
10. **Fail fast and communicate clearly** — Say immediately when something is wrong; never silently continue on a broken foundation.
