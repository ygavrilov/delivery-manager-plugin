# RFQ — Full Reference

## RFQ package (sections)

- Request summary
- Scope (in/out)
- Assumptions
- Dependencies
- Risks + mitigations
- Solution outline
- Estimate + confidence
- Timeline + milestones
- Team + ramp
- Change control
- Deliverables list

## RACI (minimal)

- Intake + qualification
    - R: DM
    - A: DM
    - C: AM/Client, SA
    - I: Eng Mgr, Finance
- Scope + acceptance
    - R: DM
    - A: Client Sponsor
    - C: SA, Tech Lead, QA Lead
    - I: Eng Mgr
- Solution outline
    - R: SA / Tech Lead
    - A: SA
    - C: DM, Security/Compliance
    - I: Client
- Estimate
    - R: Tech Lead
    - A: DM
    - C: Dev, QA, SA
    - I: AM/Client
- Staffing + schedule
    - R: DM
    - A: DM
    - C: Eng Mgr, Staffing
    - I: Client
- Commercials + submission
    - R: DM
    - A: AM
    - C: Finance, Legal
    - I: Delivery/Eng
